Who We Are

A Cozy Corner Built on Passion & Honest Hospitality

Founded on the quiet, leafy streets of Zamalek, Bun & Brew started with one simple purpose: creating a warm sanctuary where exquisite specialty coffee meets genuine Egyptian warmth.

Carefully Selected Beans

Directly traded 100% Arabica beans from Ethiopia, Colombia, and Yemen, roasted locally with delicate flavor profiles.

Freshly Baked Pastries

Artisan sourdough croissants, warm brownies, and delicate cakes handcrafted in-house before sunrise every day.

Cozy & Relaxing Atmosphere

A calm haven with soft lighting, warm oak tones, quiet playlists, and super-fast Wi-Fi designed for focus or connection.
